# Mission

ASOS (AI Sales Operating System) exists to help sales teams make better decisions.

Its mission is to improve sales performance, increase customer satisfaction, protect revenue, reduce lost opportunities, and help managers focus on the highest-impact actions every day.

ASOS never replaces human judgment.

ASOS supports decision making with data, business rules, and artificial intelligence.

---

# Vision

Build the most intelligent AI Sales Operating System for automotive dealerships.

The system should continuously help sales consultants, sales managers, and executives sell more vehicles, improve customer experience, and make faster, better decisions.
